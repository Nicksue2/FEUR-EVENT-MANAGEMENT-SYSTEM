Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I register for an event?

Go to the Dashboard, click on an event card, and press the Register button. Make sure you are logged in first.

How do I pay for a paid event?

You can pay via GCash (09615939028) or directly at the FEUR Finance Office. After paying, go to your Order List to upload your receipt.

When will I get my official QR Ticket?

For free events, you will get your QR ticket immediately in your Order List and school email. For paid events, you will receive it after the admin verifies your uploaded receipt.

Do I need to print my QR code?

No need! You can simply save a screenshot or open your Order List and show the QR code on your phone screen at the entry gate.

Why can't I click the Register button?

If the button is grayed out or red, it means you are already registered, or the event is already sold out.

Can I cancel my registration?

Yes. For free events, you can click "Cancel Ticket" in the Order List. For paid events, the cancel button is disabled to protect your payment. You must contact the admin to cancel.

How do I get a refund if I cancel a paid ticket?

We cannot process refunds directly on the website. Please use the Contact Support form below and select "Refund Request". We will check your request based on the school's refund rules. It may take a few days to process.

What happens if I forget my password?

Click "Forgot Password"on the Login page. You will receive a secure reset link in your school email.

Contact Support

Message the FEUR Events Developers. Replies will be sent to your registered email.
